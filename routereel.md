Privacy Policy

Route Reel does not require user accounts and does not intentionally collect or store personal information.

Photos access is only used when users choose to attach photos to journey moments inside their travel reels. Selected photos remain on the user’s device unless shared by the user.

The app uses Mapbox services to render maps and generate travel routes. Mapbox may process limited technical information required for map and routing functionality.

Route Reel does not sell user data or use personal data for advertising.

If analytics or additional online features are added in the future, this policy may be updated.
